# Fix dead links in generated `org/` pages and `index.md`

## Observation

After fixing relative-path resolution in `brain_links`, the largest remaining dead-link clusters are in auto-generated pages:

- `org/state.md`: 50 dead links to paths like `org/brain/adrs/explicit-approval-for-commits`
- `org/roadmap.md`: 31 dead links to paths like `org/brain/adrs/adr-live-status-widget-refresh`
- `org/options.md`: dead links to `org/brain/pitches/upstream-template-sync`
- `index.md`: 93 dead links, including links to `wiki/brain/_archive/bets/embed-pi-brain-default-behaviour`

## Why now

These pages are generated by `tools/brain-state.mjs`. The generator appears to emit stale or wrong-scope links that do not correspond to actual wiki pages. Fixing the generator fixes the dead links once and prevents them from reappearing after every `brain_sync`.

## Suggested action

1. Inspect `tools/brain-state.mjs` to see how it builds page lists and links.
2. Ensure generated links use correct relative paths and only point to existing pages.
3. Remove or correct links to `org/brain/...` scopes that do not exist.
4. Re-run `brain_links` until the auto-generated pages have zero dead links.
